Privacy Policy for Maps Tab for Qwant Extension

Last updated: 13.11.2026

Maps Tab for Qwant ("the extension") is an independent browser extension and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Qwant.

What the extension does

It adds a "Maps" tab to Qwant search result pages. When you click that tab, the extension opens your current search query in the map service you selected in the extension's settings.

Data collection

The extension does not collect, store, sell or transmit any personal data to the developer or to any third party. It contains no analytics, tracking or advertising code, and it never contacts any server operated by the developer.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (chosen map service, tab label and the "open in a new tab" option) are saved with Chrome's storage API. If Chrome Sync is turned on, Chrome may sync these settings between your own devices. The developer has no access to them.

Your search query

The extension reads the search text on the Qwant page only when you click the Maps tab, and only to build the link to your chosen map service. The query is then sent by your browser directly to that service, just as if you had typed it there yourself. That service's own privacy policy applies to what happens next.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
